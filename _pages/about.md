I work on privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs) and their deployment in large-scale, real-world systems.

Previously, I was a Senior Research Fellow at [SCRIPTS](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/scripts), Nanyang Technological University. I received my Ph.D. in Information Engineering from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/) in 2018, advised by [Prof. Sherman S. M. Chow](https://www.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/faculty/chow-sze-ming-sherman/).

My research spans applied cryptography and PETs: private set operations, privacy-preserving machine learning, searchable encryption and proofs for cloud storage, digital signatures and anonymous credentials, and privacy-preserving payment systems.
